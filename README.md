# The Front Porch

Gentle, free activities for older people living with memory loss, Alzheimer's or Parkinson's, and for the family
who sit with them. From [The Fun Shed](https://thefunshed.com).

Pick something to do from the porch: grow a garden, match the socks, or sing an old song together. There is
nothing to win or lose, no timers, no scores, no sign-up, no ads, and nothing is collected. It's made to be
enjoyed, not as a treatment.

Every piece of it is there for a reason, and the reasons are written down in [docs/research.md](docs/research.md).

Plain HTML and JavaScript, installable on a tablet or phone, works offline. MIT licence.
